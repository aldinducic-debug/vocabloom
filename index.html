Vocabloom

Grow your SAT & ACT vocabulary, a few words a day.

A free iPhone and Android app from a nonprofit. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no tracking.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

What it does

Starts at your level

A 3-minute placement quiz estimates which words you already know, so you don’t waste time on easy ones.

A few new words a day

Pick 3 to 20 words a day. Each comes with a clear definition, an example sentence, and a quick check.

Reviews that stick

Words come back after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days, right before you’d forget them.

Practice like the real test

SAT-style Words in Context questions and ACT-style commonly confused words.

On your home screen

A Word of the Day widget for iPhone and Android, plus an optional daily reminder.

Track your scores

Log practice and official test scores and see your best section scores for superscoring.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Help

Is it really free?

Yes. Vocabloom has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases, and every feature is available to everyone.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Your progress is saved on your phone.

How do I add the widget?

iPhone:

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget (or the + button).
  3. Search for Vocabloom, pick a size, and tap Add Widget. You can also add it to your Lock Screen.

Android:

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets and scroll to Vocabloom.
  3. Touch and hold Word of the Day, then drag it onto your home screen.
How do I change my test, daily goal, or test date?

Open the Settings tab and pick a new option. Words you’ve already learned are kept.

How do reminders work?

Turn on daily reminders in Settings and choose a time. Each reminder features one of that day’s words. You can turn them off anytime.

Can I track my SAT or ACT scores?

Yes. On the Progress tab, add your SAT Reading and Writing score or your ACT English and Reading scores from official or practice tests. You’ll see your best score in each section (the scores colleges that superscore look at), how much you’ve improved, and how close you are to your goal.

Can I retake the placement quiz?

Yes. Use Retake on the Progress tab, or Settings → Retake placement quiz. Your plan is rebuilt around your new level.

I got a new phone. Will my progress move over?

There are no accounts, so your progress lives only on your phone. Restoring your new phone from a backup of the old one usually brings it along.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset all progress. This erases everything on your device and can’t be undone.

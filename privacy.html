Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Vocabloom is a free study app made by a nonprofit. We built it to collect no personal information at all.

What we collect

Nothing. Vocabloom has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking tools. The app works without an internet connection and doesn't send any data to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • the test you chose, your test timeframe, and your daily goal
  • your placement quiz result
  • which words you've learned, your review schedule, and your quiz answers
  • your study streak and activity history
  • any test scores and score goals you choose to enter
  • your settings, such as reminder time and appearance

This information never leaves your device, and we can't see it. You can erase it anytime with Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Backups

If your phone backs up to iCloud or Google, your progress may be included in that backup. Apple and Google handle those backups under their own privacy policies; we never receive them.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. There is no notification server, and we can't see whether you turn reminders on.

Home-screen widget

The widget shows words from your study plan. The app shares those words with the widget through storage on your device that only Vocabloom and its widget can read.

Children and teens

Vocabloom is designed for high school students. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update it and the date above. We will never start collecting personal information without clearly asking first.
